How to actually find these numbers

This tool ranks value — it doesn't pull live award seats. No free, legal API covers real-time availability across every partner (that's the paid product point.me and Seats.aero sell). Check these yourself, then plug the numbers in above:

Cash priceGoogle Flights or the airline's own site for the identical route/cabin/dates.
Award spaceLog into the partner's own site with a dummy or real frequent-flyer account (united.com, aircanada.com, flyingblue.com, ba.com, singaporeair.com) — most accurate saver-space source.
Cross-checkseats.aero's free web search or point.me's free tier (15 results/search) to sanity-check space across programs at once.
Before transferringConfirm the seat is still there in the partner account. Chase transfers are instant but final — never transfer speculatively.
